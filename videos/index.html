Alchemy > ZBrush: Sketching through Alchemy generated alpha masks

A quick demo showing how an Alchemy sketch can be used inside ZBrush. Using the Alchemy sketch as an alpha mask, flat shapes are explored in 3d for a helmet or mech head concept piece. This same process can be applied to other object types such as spaceships, swords, armour etc… By inverse catheter.



Andrew Jones Live Visuals

A video featuring Andrew Jones doing live visuals with Alchemy at the Autodesk Siggraph opening party. An incredible party as we have come to expect from Massive Black & ConceptArt.org, held at the Shrine Auditorium, venue of the Academy Awards until 2002. Special thanks to Andrew Jones and Jason Manley for making it all happen. Soundtrack by The Glitch Mob.
